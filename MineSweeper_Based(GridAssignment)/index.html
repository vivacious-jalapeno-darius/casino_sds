Betting

There is a slider that can be used to change the bet amount. NOTE: when your total cash amount is (-), then the max amount to bet is only $1. PLAY AT YOUR OWN RISK

Gambling

Grid

Behind the mystery boxes is either a REWARD

Or a FLASHBANG. There is a 90% chance that it is a reward

Unlike regular Mine Sweeper, the goal is to get as many rewards as possible WITHOUT getting a flashbang. Click on a mystery box to reveal.

Multiplier

The multiplier multiplies the bet amount, but the multiplier value increases after every successful click. For example the original bet value was $50, after getting a reward, the bet value will multiply with the multiplier value(1.05). Then the multiplier amount will multiply by itself (1.05*1.05), which will set the new multiplier amount to 1.1025. Next round, if clicked on the reward, the new bet value ($52.5) will multiply with the new multiplier amount (1.1025). This process keeps repeating.

If clicked on a flashbang, then the bet amount multiplies with the multiplier. Then it subtracts that amount from the total cash.

Cash Out

If you satisfied by the earnings and don't want to risk losing, then you can click the CASH OUT button, to keep your earnings. This will teleport you back to the Betting page, where you can bet with your new cash value.
