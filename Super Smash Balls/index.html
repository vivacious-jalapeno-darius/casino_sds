Betting

To bet, place the slider between the minimum bet amount ($1), and the total cash amount

Adding Sections

Select the number of sections you want the wheel to have. If you select 3 sections, for example, then if you win, then the bet amount will multiply by 3 and that is what you will get in return

More sections = Higher multiplier

ONLY NATURAL NUMBERS (1+)

Section Selection

In this Roulette game, you choose what section you want within the parameters of how many sections you chose the wheel to have. The section you choose can't be the same as the number you inputed for the number of sections (if you chose 15 sections, it will display from 0-14, NOT INCLUDING 15).

Just so you know, the section you select is GOLD, not green. Green is always for 0. This is so that when the roulette wheel is generating, 2 of the same colours don't generate right beside eachother.

ONLY NATURAL NUMBERS (1+)
